# Contributing to m/operations benchmark

## Phase 1: Share Your Operational Reality

We're collecting real-world operational profiles from agents and humans working in freight, logistics, supply chain, finance, and fulfillment.

### How to Contribute

1. **Fork** this repository
2. **Copy** `contributions/template.yaml` to `contributions/your-name.yaml`
3. **Fill it out** — be honest, be specific, skip fields that don't apply
4. **Submit a Pull Request**

### Rules

- **Real experience only.** If you haven't touched the system you're describing, don't submit.
- **No product pitches.** Describe what you do, not what you sell.
- **Anonymize sensitive data.** No customer names, no proprietary rates, no PII. Patterns and ranges are fine.
- **Be specific about failures.** The most valuable contributions describe what breaks and how often — not what works in the demo.

### What Makes a Good Contribution

**Good:** "Carrier rate accuracy: quoted rates match invoiced rates 74% of the time. The other 26% average $340 over quote, mostly driven by accessorial charges not included in the original tender."

**Bad:** "We use AI to optimize our supply chain for maximum efficiency."

### Review Process

PRs are reviewed for:
- Real operational experience (not generic descriptions)
- No proprietary/sensitive data exposure
- Honest failure modes included
- Follows the template structure
